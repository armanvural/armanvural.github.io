Terms of Use

Alexandria Personal is software for its owner's personal account workflows. It is not a public email service.

Authorized use

Only the configured account owner may connect and use the integration. Google permissions are granted through OAuth and can be revoked at any time. Connecting an account does not authorize arbitrary outgoing messages: each send must be within the owner's instructions for its recipient and purpose.

Availability and responsibility

Google API availability, account restrictions, token revocation and network conditions can interrupt the integration. It does not guarantee uninterrupted access or email delivery. The owner should review recipients, message content and important outcomes. Ambiguous send results require checking Sent before any retry.

Privacy

The Privacy Policy describes Google data access, storage, AI-assistant processing and removal of access. This information website does not collect Google credentials or provide a public account-registration flow.
